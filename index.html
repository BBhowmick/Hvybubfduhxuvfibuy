Welcome to Your Mountain Home

Tucked away in the quiet, picturesque landscape of Ghalaytor Bazar, Sittong, our homestay is designed to be exactly what its name promises—a gharoa (home-like) escape where travel never ends. Wake up to golden sunrises over the majestic peaks of Mt. Kanchenjunga, sip hot tea while looking out at the lush terraced valleys, and experience true local mountain hospitality.
